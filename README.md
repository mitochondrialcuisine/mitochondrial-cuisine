## What this is

Mitochondrial Cuisine is a culinary discipline that organizes the design of food
around the mitochondrion — the organelle responsible for cellular energy and
metabolic signaling — rather than treating food as fuel alone. This repository is
the timestamped, version-controlled record of the framework: the founding treatise,
the five foundations, the design principles, the working grammar, and the Honest Line.

## The Honest Line

This is a **culinary theory**, not medicine. Nothing in this work diagnoses, treats,
cures, or prevents any disease. The peer-reviewed science referenced throughout is
attributed to the researchers who produced it; the framework, principles, and the
Honest Line are the original work of the founders, Luke Wu & Reanne Wu, and are the
property of Crucian Farms.
