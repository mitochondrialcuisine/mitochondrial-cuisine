# Scientists, Institutions & The Wider Field

Inclusion is attribution or context, never endorsement of Mitochondrial Cuisine.

## Scientists and clinical translators

- **Douglas C. Wallace** — Children's Hospital of Philadelphia · University of Pennsylvania. Founder of human mitochondrial genetics. Established the maternal inheritance of mitochondrial DNA and the mitochondrial paradigm of metabolic and degenerative disease, aging, and cancer.
- **Vamsi K. Mootha** — Harvard Medical School · Broad Institute · HHMI. Mitochondrial systems biology. Built MitoCarta, the reference inventory of the mammalian mitochondrial proteome, turning the organelle into a systematically mappable system.
- **Navdeep S. Chandel** — Northwestern University. Mitochondrial ROS as signal. Showed that mitochondria-derived reactive oxygen species act as deliberate signaling messengers, not merely damage — the mechanistic heart of hormesis.
- **Bruce M. Spiegelman** — Dana-Farber · Harvard Medical School. Mitochondrial biogenesis. Discovered PGC-1α, the master transcriptional coactivator that orchestrates the building of new mitochondria and adaptive thermogenesis.
- **Martin Picard** — Columbia University. Mitochondrial psychobiology. Reframed the mitochondrion from powerhouse to cellular processor, integrating metabolic, behavioral, and psychosocial inputs into systemic signals.
- **Johan Auwerx** — EPFL, Lausanne. Mitophagy & urolithin A. Demonstrated that urolithin A activates mitophagy and extends healthspan in model organisms, carrying the finding toward human trials.
- **Eric Verdin** — Buck Institute for Research on Aging. Ketone-body signaling. Showed that β-hydroxybutyrate is an endogenous histone-deacetylase inhibitor — a fuel molecule that doubles as an epigenetic signal.
- **Valter Longo** — University of Southern California. Fasting-mimicking diet. Developed and trialed the fasting-mimicking diet, the strongest randomized evidence that structured eating shifts metabolic markers and biological age.
- **Satchidananda Panda** — Salk Institute for Biological Studies. Circadian & time-restricted eating. Established that the timing of eating — independent of calories — reshapes metabolic health through the body's circadian and fuel-switching machinery.
- **Frank Madeo** — University of Graz. Autophagy & spermidine. Showed that the dietary polyamine spermidine induces autophagy and extends lifespan — a food-borne route into cellular clearance.
- **Carlos López-Otín** — University of Oviedo. Hallmarks of aging. Co-authored the hallmarks-of-aging framework, in which mitochondrial dysfunction sits among the defining, interconnected drivers of aging.
- **Rhonda Patrick, PhD** — FoundMyFitness · formerly CHORI, with Bruce Ames. Micronutrients, vitamin D & omega-3 signaling. Published in the FASEB Journal with Bruce Ames on the mechanism by which vitamin D and the marine omega-3s regulate serotonin synthesis, alongside work on micronutrient deficiency and DNA damage. Her public teaching popularizes that same literature.
- **Peter Attia, MD** — Early Medical · formerly NIH National Cancer Institute. Longevity, metabolic health & muscle. Trained as a surgeon and published peer-reviewed oncology and immunotherapy research at the NCI before moving into longevity practice. His metabolic-health-and-muscle framing is synthesis of the literature rather than his own primary science.
- **William W. Li, MD** — Angiogenesis Foundation · Harvard-trained physician. Angiogenesis & antiangiogenic dietary factors. Published peer-reviewed work on angiogenesis, wound healing, and diet-derived antiangiogenic compounds — including 'Tumor angiogenesis as a target for dietary cancer prevention' — which his books and talks then carry to a wide audience.
- **Thomas N. Seyfried** — Boston College. Metabolic theory of cancer (contested). Argues the contested hypothesis that cancer is fundamentally a mitochondrial metabolic disease — named here as active scientific debate, never as a basis for any claim.

## Educators and popularizers

- **Stan Efferding** — The Vertical Diet · world-record powerlifter. Whole foods, digestibility & micronutrient density. Built a practitioner's system around easily digested whole foods and micronutrient density as the engine of elite strength. Field-tested practice, not peer-reviewed research.
- **Vladimir Shmondenko (Anatoly)** — Ukrainian powerlifter · online educator. Simple whole-food eating for real strength. The 'gym janitor' who out-lifts everyone on camera, powered by a plain, collagen-rich whole-food diet. Included as culture and demonstration — quiet strength over theatrical salt — never as evidence.
- **Dr. Eric Berg, DC** — Doctor of Chiropractic · health educator & author. Popular keto & intermittent-fasting education. One of the most-followed communicators on ketogenic eating, fasting, and cellular energy, translating metabolic concepts for a mass audience. A clinician-educator, not a primary researcher — his popular claims are framed here as outreach, and any specific assertion must still trace back to the cited peer-reviewed literature to enter the canon.
- **Thomas DeLauer** — YouTube educator · nutrition coach. Fasting, autophagy & mitochondrial biogenesis explainers. One of the largest YouTube channels reading fasting, autophagy, AMPK, and mitochondrial-biogenesis papers on camera for a general audience. Valuable as a bridge to the literature — the citations he reads are the evidence, not the video.
- **Paul Saladino, MD** — Animal-based diet advocate · physician. Animal-based eating & plant-toxin skepticism. Argues an animal-based diet on grounds of nutrient density and plant antinutrients, at very large scale online. Included as a prominent and openly contested position — much of it runs ahead of the evidence, and none of it enters the canon without a cited primary source.
- **Siim Land** — Author & YouTube educator · Estonia. Metabolic autophagy, longevity & mitochondrial hormesis. Writes and broadcasts intensively on autophagy, mTOR/AMPK cycling, and mitochondrial hormesis, summarising longevity papers for a large audience. A synthesist rather than a researcher — useful as an index into the literature.

## Organizations and research institutions

- **Amazentis / Timeline** — Company · Lausanne. Urolithin A (Mitopure). Spun out of EPFL research to develop urolithin A into a clinically studied mitophagy activator.
- **L-Nutra** — Company · Los Angeles. Fasting-mimicking nutrition (ProLon). Founded around Valter Longo's fasting-mimicking-diet research to translate the protocol into a food program.
- **Stealth BioTherapeutics** — Company · Massachusetts. Mitochondria-targeted therapeutics. Develops peptides such as elamipretide aimed at the inner mitochondrial membrane in rare mitochondrial disease.
- **Elysium Health** — Company · New York. NAD⁺ metabolism. Commercializes NAD⁺-precursor formulations rooted in sirtuin and cellular-energy research.
- **Pretzel Therapeutics** — Company · Sweden / US. Mitochondrial DNA & biology. Drug-discovery company targeting mitochondrial genome regulation and quality control.
- **MitoQ** — Company · New Zealand. Mitochondria-targeted antioxidant. Develops a mitochondria-targeted coenzyme-Q compound studied for oxidative-stress signaling.
- **Broad Institute** — Research institute · Cambridge, MA. MitoCarta & mitochondrial genomics. Home of the MitoCarta inventory and large-scale mitochondrial systems biology.
- **Salk Institute** — Research institute · La Jolla, CA. Circadian metabolism & mtDNA stress. Center for time-restricted-eating and mitochondrial-signaling research.
- **Buck Institute** — Research institute · Novato, CA. Aging & bioenergetics. Dedicated aging-research institute studying ketone signaling, mitophagy, and proton leak.
- **Karolinska Institutet** — Research institute · Stockholm. mtDNA & aging. Long-standing center for mitochondrial-genome and aging research.

## Measurement hardware

- **Dexcom** — Company · San Diego. Continuous glucose monitoring (Stelo). Built the continuous glucose sensor into a consumer instrument, including Stelo, an over-the-counter CGM for people not on insulin. Continuous glucose is the closest thing to a live readout of how a meal is actually handled — the substrate signal the framework reads rather than guesses at.
- **Abbott / FreeStyle Libre** — Company · Chicago. OTC metabolic CGM (Lingo). The FreeStyle Libre sensor platform underwrites much of the world's CGM data, and Lingo extends it to a non-diabetic, metabolic-awareness audience. Its long clinical record is what makes consumer glucose data trustworthy enough to reason from.
- **Levels Health** — Company · New York. CGM software & interpretation layer. A software layer over third-party sensors that turns raw glucose traces into meal-by-meal patterns. It matters here because interpretation, not the sensor, is where a number becomes a cooking decision.
- **Lumen (MetaFlow Ltd.)** — Company · Tel Aviv. Handheld CO₂ breath analysis for fuel switching. A handheld device that infers which fuel the body is burning from the CO₂ concentration of a single breath — a consumer proxy for respiratory quotient. Validation work against metabolic carts has appeared in peer-reviewed venues including FASEB and JMIR reports (2023–24), making metabolic flexibility something a cook can actually observe.
- **KORR Medical Technologies** — Company · Salt Lake City. Indirect calorimetry — VO₂ max & RMR. Makes the CardioCoach line of indirect-calorimetry hardware measuring VO₂ max and resting metabolic rate. This is the clinical-grade metabolic cart at a price point clinics and gyms can reach, so ground-truth substrate measurement isn't confined to research labs.
- **DexaFit / DEXA Body** — Franchise clinics · United States. DEXA body composition, VO₂ max & RMR access. The clinic layer that puts DEXA body-composition scanning, VO₂ max testing, and RMR measurement within walking distance of ordinary people. Access is the contribution: composition and expenditure are the denominators every dietary claim quietly depends on.
- **Oura Ring** — Company · Helsinki. Validated sleep & heart-rate variability. A ring-form sensor for sleep staging, nocturnal heart rate, HRV, and temperature. A 2025 peer-reviewed comparison study placed it among the strongest validated consumer sleep and HRV devices — the recovery half of the picture that food alone can't report.
- **Ultrahuman** — Company · Bangalore. Ring AIR paired with M1 CGM. Pairs a smart ring with an Abbott-sensor-based CGM so glucose response and recovery are read in one place, and publishes its own validation studies. The pairing is the point: a substrate signal is far more legible next to sleep and HRV.
- **WHOOP** — Company · Boston. Strain, recovery & sleep debt. A screenless band built around a daily strain-and-recovery loop with sleep-debt accounting. It quantifies the demand side — what the day actually cost — which is the context any meal is answering.
- **Hume Band** — Company · United States. Screen-free metabolic-capacity wearable. A deliberately screen-free band tracking HRV, sleep, and skin temperature toward a single metabolic-capacity picture, with its 2.0 generation launched in 2025. The restraint is notable: fewer numbers, aimed at trend rather than moment-to-moment reaction.
