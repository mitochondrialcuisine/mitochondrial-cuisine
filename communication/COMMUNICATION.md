# The Rewiring — Communication

> Once the machinery is fed, the operator is rewired. Metabolic restoration returns a nervous system to safety — and a nervous system in safety can finally afford to be honest. The second half of the discipline is learning to speak from that regulated state, not from the survival wiring that scarcity, stress, and inflammation kept switched on.

## The Arc

### 01 · Fed first

You cannot reason your way out of a dysregulated body. Stable substrate, stable energy, and low inflammation give the nervous system the ground it needs. Communication work begins where the metabolic work leaves off — not before.

### 02 · Authenticity over attachment

Gabor Maté names the child's original bargain: when authenticity threatens connection, we suppress the authentic self to keep the bond. Decades later that suppression speaks for us — as appeasement, as reactivity, as silence. Healing is the slow permission to be authentic and connected at once.

### 03 · The body keeps the tab

Unspoken needs do not disappear; they are paid for in the body. Maté's central observation — that chronic stress and self-abandonment register physiologically — closes the loop with the cuisine. How you eat calms the body; how you speak keeps it calm.

### 04 · Rewire the sentence

Marshall Rosenberg's Nonviolent Communication is the executable grammar for the healed operator: observe without evaluating, feel without blaming, name the need beneath the feeling, and make a request instead of a demand. Four moves, endlessly repeatable.

## Nonviolent Communication

| Step | Prompt | Reactive form | Honest form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Observation | What actually happened, stripped of story? | You always ignore me. | The last three times I spoke, you were looking at your phone. |
| Feeling | What am I feeling — not what I think you did? | You make me feel worthless. | I feel lonely and a little unimportant. |
| Need | What universal need is alive under the feeling? | You need to respect me. | I need connection and to matter to the people I'm with. |
| Request | What concrete, present request would meet it? | Stop being so rude. | Would you be willing to put the phone down while we talk for ten minutes? |
