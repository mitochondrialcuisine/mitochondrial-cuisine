# The Working Grammar

A compound-to-pathway reference: what a compound is, where a kitchen finds it, the
pathway it engages, an honest grade of the evidence, and the culinary handle by
which a chef controls it. No entry asserts that a dish treats, cures, or prevents
disease.

## Evidence grades

- **Established** — Well-characterized and multiply confirmed, including human data.
- **Emerging** — Strong preclinical and early human data; causal human proof still thin.
- **Mixed** — The literature is genuinely divided; the honest entry says so rather than choosing a side.
- **Contested** — Genuinely contested at the level of causation; named as a boundary, never resolved for marketing's sake.
- **Category** — A technique-level pattern (e.g. fermentation, char) rather than a single isolated compound.

## Techniques

| Compound | Source | Pathway | Grade | Culinary handle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Char / Maillard | Live fire, controlled searing | Mild hormetic aromatic compounds | Category | Enough for signal and flavor; controlled to avoid the excess. |
| Fermentation | Ferments, cultured dairy, aged elements | Organic acids; microbial polyphenol conversion | Category | A second kitchen the microbiome runs. |
| Cut-and-rest | Alliums, brassicas cut before cooking | Enzymatic formation window before heat | Category | The knife starts the reaction; rest lets it finish. |
| Gentle heat | Low-temperature cooking | Preserves heat-labile enzymes & vitamins | Category | Stay below the denaturation threshold for the target compound. |
