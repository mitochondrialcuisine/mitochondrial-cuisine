# Provenance

**Mitochondrial Cuisine** is an original culinary discipline founded in 2026 by
Luke Wu & Reanne Wu. It is the property of **Crucian Farms**, its parent company
(Christiansted · St. Croix · U.S. Virgin Islands), under which it is stewarded by
the Mitochondrial Cuisine Foundation.

Its canonical origin — the founding treatise, the five foundations, the design
principles, the working grammar, and the Honest Line — is published at
https://mitochondrialcuisine.com.

## Evidence chain

1. **Public indexing** — the canonical site is crawled and indexed, creating a
   dated public record of first appearance.
2. **Internet Archive** — key pages are submitted to the Wayback Machine for an
   immutable third-party timestamp.
3. **Version control** — this repository records when each element of the work
   first existed.
4. **Academic anchoring** — a DOI (via Zenodo) places the treatise in the formal
   scholarly record.
5. **Bidirectional cross-linking** — external publications link back to the
   canonical treatise, and the site links out.
